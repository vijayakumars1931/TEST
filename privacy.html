Privacy policy.

Last updated · 16 November 2026

01 The short version

We collect only what we need to plan and deliver your event. We don't sell your data, we don't spam, and you can ask us to delete your information at any time.

02 What we collect

When you submit an enquiry or subscribe to The Lookbook, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and city.
  • Event details you choose to share — type, date, guest count, budget range and notes.
  • Basic, anonymous analytics about how this website is used, if analytics is enabled.

03 How we use it

To respond to your enquiry, prepare proposals, coordinate vendors, deliver contracted services and — if you opt in — send occasional Lookbook inspiration. We may contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email using the details you provide.

04 Who we share it with

Only the people who need it to deliver your event: venues, vendors and our internal crew, under confidentiality. We may use trusted service providers for hosting, email and analytics. We never sell personal data to third parties.

05 How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for up to 24 months so we can pick up the conversation. Event and financial records are retained as required by Indian tax law. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe.

06 Your choices

You can request a copy, correction or deletion of your personal data by writing to hello@spadeeventzz.com. You can unsubscribe from marketing at any time using the link in any email or by replying "stop" to a WhatsApp message.

07 Cookies & third parties

This site is a static website. It may load Google Fonts, and if analytics is enabled, a privacy-friendly analytics tool. Any embedded maps, forms or social plugins are governed by their own privacy policies.

08 Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@spadeeventzz.com or write to Spadeeventzz, Studio 04, Road No. 36, Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad 500033, India.
